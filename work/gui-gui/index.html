Challenge

The situation

Gui Gui is a beauty-inspired slime priced at $14. It was launching into a crowded holiday toy aisle where competitors sold for under $10.

The goal

Launch at a premium price, without discounting, and win the holiday season.

Our impact

Win the space before launch, and explain the price before shoppers reach the shelf.

01 · Retail plan

Own the display, not the aisle.

I built a joint business plan with Walmart buyers that secured first-to-market, out-of-aisle endcaps, so Gui Gui stood apart from the slime aisle.

  • Joint business plan with Walmart
  • First-to-market endcaps
02 · Creators

Creators, not coupons.

Instead of discounting, we led with creator education through mega-influencers like Ongsquad, so kids knew why it was worth $14.

  • Creator partnerships led by Ongsquad
  • Content that sold the experience
03 · Timing

Hit the feed the week stock hit the store.

Custom unboxing mailers were timed to warehouse dates, sending shoppers straight to the displays.

  • Unboxing mailers synced to stock dates
  • Digital traffic pointed at the display
The result
96%sell-through in 5 weeks
#2toy item in Canada
5xreturn on earned media

Gui Gui sold through 96% in five weeks at full price, became the #2 toy item in Canada, and earned a 5x return on earned media.

What this means for your brand

A premium price needs a reason shoppers understand before they reach the shelf. Time your creator push to the week product lands.
