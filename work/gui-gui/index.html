Moose Toys · Case study

Gui Gui

How we launched a $14 slime into an under-$10 holiday aisle with no discounts, and sold through 96% in five weeks.

96%sell-through in 5 weeks
#2toy item in Canada
5xreturn on earned media
01 · The challenge

A premium price in a discount aisle.

Gui Gui is a beauty-inspired slime priced at $14. It was launching into a crowded holiday toy aisle where competitors sold for under $10.

  • Commoditized, price-driven category
  • Crowded holiday shelf
  • No room to compete on discounts
02 · The retail plan

Win the space before launch.

I brought commerce upstream and built a joint business plan with Walmart buyers. That secured first-to-market, out-of-aisle endcaps so Gui Gui stood apart from the slime aisle.

  • Joint business plan with Walmart
  • First-to-market timing
  • Out-of-aisle endcap displays
03 · Creators, not coupons

Explain the price before they reach the shelf.

Instead of discounting, we leaned into creator-first education with mega-influencers like Ongsquad. Custom unboxing mailers were timed to the week stock arrived, driving shoppers straight to the displays.

  • Creator partnerships led by Ongsquad
  • Unboxing mailers synced to warehouse dates
  • Digital traffic pointed at the in-store display
04 · The results

Sold through in five weeks.

The premium price held, and the displays emptied fast.

96%sell-through in 5 weeks
#2toy item in Canada
5xreturn on earned media

What this means for your brand

A premium price needs a reason shoppers understand before they reach the shelf. Time your creator push to the week product lands.
