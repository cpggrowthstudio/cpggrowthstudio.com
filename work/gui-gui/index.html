Moose Toys · Disrupting a commodity market

Gui Gui

96%sell-through in 5 weeks
#2toy item in Canada
5xreturn on earned media

The problem

We needed to launch a premium-priced ($14) beauty-inspired slime into a commoditized, saturated holiday market where competitors priced under $10.

The steps

  1. I brought commerce upstream, building a joint business plan with Walmart buyers to secure first-to-market, out-of-aisle endcaps.
  2. Instead of relying on discounts, we leaned into creator-first education, partnering with mega-influencers like Ongsquad.
  3. We synced custom unboxing mailers with warehouse delivery dates, driving educated digital traffic directly to the physical retail displays.
Video: TikTok creator campaign highlight reel
Image: Walmart out-of-aisle display

The result

We achieved 96% retail sell-through of our out-of-aisle displays in just 5 weeks. Gui Gui became the #2 toy item in Canada, delivering a 5x return on earned media investment.

What this means for your brand

A premium price needs a reason shoppers already understand before they reach the shelf. Time your creator and digital push to the week product lands, not launch day.
