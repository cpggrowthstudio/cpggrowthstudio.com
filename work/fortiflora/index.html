Nestlé Purina / FortiFlora

FortiFlora more than doubled and became #1 in both vet and retail.

Business case, positioning, multichannel launch
100%+brand growth
#1share in vet and retail

Challenge

The situation

FortiFlora was a trusted vet-channel probiotic, but everyday pet parents barely knew it. A new global innovation, FortiFlora PRO, was ready, but leadership was hesitant to launch it in Canada. Competitors were entering the space, and we needed to own the market before they did.

The goal

Convince leadership to move from a vet-only model to multichannel: take FortiFlora into pet specialty and retail, and launch PRO as a vet exclusive.

Our impact

Insight-led, consumer-first messaging, backed by a business case leadership could say yes to.

01 · The business case

Prove the money before asking for the move.

I built the business case and financial models showing the revenue potential of new channels and the everyday pet owner.

  • Channel-by-channel revenue model
  • Leadership sign-off on the multichannel plan
02 · Positioning

Two products, two clear jobs.

To protect the vets, the most efficacious formula, PRO, stayed vet-exclusive. FortiFlora went to retail with consumer-friendly reasons to buy: gut health and science-backed nutrition. Regulatory, legal and vet affairs helped simplify the technical claims and kept us on time.

  • FortiFlora PRO: vet exclusive
  • FortiFlora: gut health for every pet owner
03 · Sales & campaign

Win the shelf, then fill the cart.

Sales got clinical detailers and launch promos to secure distribution and manage the transition. For pet specialty and eCommerce, I built a 360 campaign with our TV, digital and eCommerce teams.

  • Clinical detailers and launch promos
  • 360 campaign: TV, digital, eCommerce
The result
100%+brand growth
#1share in vet and retail
0loss of vet sales

FortiFlora grew more than 100% and reached #1 share in both channels, with a successful PetSmart launch and no loss of vet sales. It also paved the way for new supplements like Calming Care and Joint Care.

What this means for your brand

Moving from vets or independents into mass and Amazon? Give each channel a distinct job, and consider a channel-exclusive SKU, before you launch.
