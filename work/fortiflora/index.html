FortiFlora

+107%overall brand growth
#1share position in retail
0erosion in vet clinic sales

The problem

FortiFlora was the #1 trusted probiotic in veterinary clinics, but it suffered from low consumer awareness and was missing large volume opportunities in specialty retail and eCommerce.

The steps

  1. I took P&L ownership of the $100M portfolio and set distinct channel roles to prevent conflict.
  2. We partnered with regulatory teams to simplify technical clinical claims for everyday pet parents.
  3. To protect our foundational veterinary partners, I launched FortiFlora PRO as a highly efficacious, vet-exclusive SKU.
Video: omnichannel strategy presentation
Image: PetSmart endcap display vs. vet clinic detailer

The result

This bifurcated channel strategy delivered +107% overall brand growth, capturing the #1 market share position in retail with zero erosion to core veterinary clinic sales.

What this means for your brand

If you sell through vets, specialty or independents and are moving into mass or Amazon, give each channel a distinct job before you launch. A channel-exclusive SKU or pack can keep your founding partners loyal while you scale.
