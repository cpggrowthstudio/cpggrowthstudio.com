Nestlé Purina · Case study

FortiFlora

How we took the #1 vet probiotic into PetSmart and Amazon, more than doubled the brand, and kept every vet clinic on side.

+107%brand growth
#1share in retail
0loss of vet sales
01 · The challenge

Loved by vets. Unknown to pet owners.

FortiFlora was the #1 probiotic recommended in vet clinics. But most pet owners had never heard of it, and the brand was missing huge volume in specialty retail and on Amazon.

  • Strong vet trust, low consumer awareness
  • No real presence in pet specialty or eCommerce
  • Risk: going wide could anger the vets who built the brand
02 · The strategy

Give every channel its own job.

I took ownership of the $100M P&L and set clear roles for each channel, so retail growth would add to vet sales instead of stealing from them.

  • Vet clinics: professional recommendation and the strongest formula
  • Pet specialty and mass: awareness and trial
  • Amazon: convenience and repeat purchase
03 · The execution

Simple claims for retail. A PRO version for vets.

We worked with regulatory to turn clinical language into claims a pet owner understands in seconds. To protect our vet partners, I launched FortiFlora PRO, a vet-exclusive formula they could still own.

  • Simplified, compliant consumer claims
  • PetSmart endcaps and in-store displays
  • FortiFlora PRO launched as vet-only
04 · The results

More than doubled the brand. Zero vet erosion.

The split-channel strategy delivered growth everywhere at once.

+107%brand growth
#1share in retail
0loss of vet sales

What this means for your brand

Moving from vets or independents into mass and Amazon? Give each channel a distinct job, and consider a channel-exclusive SKU, before you launch.
