EssentialCare

How a fast rebrand and an automated CRM journey turned a declining vet brand into the #2 player in its category.

+10.1share points from rebrand
+6share points from CRM
#2in the category
01 · The challenge

A trusted vet brand losing ground.

EssentialCare was a mature, vet-exclusive wellness line. Newer brands were pulling pet owners away into specialty stores, and share was slipping every quarter.

  • Mature brand with dated packaging
  • Key health benefits buried on the back of pack
  • No way to keep new puppy and kitten owners coming back
02 · The rebrand

Put the proof on the front.

I led a fast, data-driven rebrand. The most important claims, urinary and gut health, moved to the front of the pack so pet owners and vet staff saw them instantly.

  • New packaging system across the line
  • Urinary and microbiome claims front and centre
  • Launched with a national TV spot
03 · Trial to CRM

Win them at the first vet visit.

Early trial decides long-term loyalty. So we handed out puppy and kitten starter kits at the first vet visit, then connected each kit to an automated digital journey with rebates for repeat purchases on clinic web stores.

  • Starter kits at the first vet visit
  • Automated milestone emails and rebates
  • Repeat purchases routed back to the clinic
04 · The results

From losing share to #2 in the category.

The rebrand and CRM program each moved share on their own, and together they strengthened loyalty with vet partners.

+10.1share points from rebrand
+6share points from CRM
#2in the category

What this means for your brand

Your best proof is often hiding on the back of the pack. And trial without a follow-up journey is money left on the table.
