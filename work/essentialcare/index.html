Nestlé Purina · Brand turnaround and CRM lifecycle

EssentialCare

+10.1share points from the rebrand
+6share points from CRM
#2player in the category

i.The problem

Our mature, vet-exclusive wellness line was losing market share to modern competitors pulling buyers into pet specialty stores.

ii.The steps

  1. I executed a rapid, data-driven rebrand, moving critical urinary and microbiome claims to the front of the pack.
  2. Knowing early trial dictates long-term retention, I deployed puppy and kitten starter kits handed out at the first vet visit.
  3. We connected this physical trial to an automated digital CRM journey, offering online rebates for repeat purchases on local clinic web stores.
Image: before and after packaging overhaul
Video: automated CRM flowchart

iii.The result

The rebrand alone added +10.1 market share points, and the automated CRM retention program added another +6 share points, moving the brand to the #2 player in the category while strengthening B2B loyalty.

What this means for your brand

Your strongest proof is often hiding on the back of the pack. And trial without a follow-up journey is money left on the table: capture the buyer at first use and give them a reason to come back.
