Hill's Science Diet

+220%Amazon sales growth
28% → 40%Subscribe & Save adoption
6months to get there

The problem

The Canadian Science Diet portfolio was underperforming on Amazon compared to the US market, due to a lack of dedicated digital strategy and a previously failed launch.

The steps

  1. I built the Amazon strategy from scratch, starting with a comprehensive digital shelf audit and A+ content optimization.
  2. I partnered directly with Amazon SVS to launch targeted paid search campaigns.
  3. I optimized promotional incentives to prioritize recurring revenue models over one-off discounts.
Image: digital shelf audit and A+ content optimization

The result

This drove +220% Amazon sales growth and lifted Subscribe & Save adoption from 28% to 40% in six months, establishing a playbook adopted as a national best practice.

What this means for your brand

Every point of autoship or Subscribe & Save is revenue you don't pay to win again. Promote the subscription, not the one-time discount.
