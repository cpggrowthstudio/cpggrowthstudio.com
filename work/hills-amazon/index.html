Hill's Science Diet

How we rebuilt Hill's Science Diet on Amazon Canada from scratch and created a playbook adopted nationally.

+220%Amazon sales growth
40%Subscribe & Save, up from 28%
6months
01 · The challenge

A failed launch and no real plan.

Science Diet in Canada was far behind its US performance on Amazon. An earlier launch had failed and there was no dedicated digital strategy.

  • Weak listings and content
  • No search advertising plan
  • Promotions built on one-off discounts
02 · The audit

Fix the digital shelf first.

I led a full digital shelf audit, then rebuilt the product pages with stronger titles, images and A+ content so shoppers could see why the brand is worth it.

  • Full digital shelf audit
  • Rebuilt titles, images and A+ content
  • Clear benefit-led product pages
03 · The growth engine

Ads that find buyers. Promos that keep them.

We partnered with Amazon to launch targeted search campaigns, and shifted promotions away from one-off discounts toward Subscribe & Save.

  • Targeted search ads with Amazon
  • Promotions rebuilt around Subscribe & Save
  • Focus on recurring revenue
04 · The results

Growth that keeps coming back.

Within six months the program became the national best practice.
