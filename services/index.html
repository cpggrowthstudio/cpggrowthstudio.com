FAQ

Who do you work with?

Pet and CPG brands, usually $2M to $25M in sales, selling or about to sell into big retailers or Amazon.

What happens on the free call?

30 minutes on your brand and your biggest problem. You leave with two or three things I'd do first. No pitch unless you ask.

Can you get us in front of retail buyers?

Yes. Retail Launch pairs me with a retail broker partner who handles buyer intros and account follow-up. His commission is included in the 7% sales share, so there's no separate broker fee. I build the pitch, the pricing and the plan to sell through, so the listing sticks.

Are we too small for a broker?

Many large brokers want $2M to $3M in sales before they'll take you on. Retail Launch is built for brands below that line.

What happens after the first 6 months?

Most brands keep going. Keep it growing covers promos, line reviews and the next retailer from $1,500 a month, or you can move up to Fractional CMO. The sales share runs for 24 months on each retailer we open.

Do you replace my agency?

No. I lead the strategy and direct your agencies so they work to one plan.

How fast can we start?

An audit can usually start within two weeks.
