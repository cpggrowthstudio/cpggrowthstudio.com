How to grow Amazon Subscribe & Save for a pet brand

AhmedEx-Nestlé Purina, Hill's, Moose Toys · Updated November 3, 2026
Short answer

Fix your digital shelf first, then buy traffic, then reward subscribing instead of one-off deals. At Hill's Science Diet in Canada, that order grew Amazon sales 200% and lifted Subscribe & Save from 28% to 40%.

Why Subscribe & Save matters for pet brands

Pet food, treats and supplements are bought on a schedule. Every Subscribe & Save customer is revenue you don't have to pay to win again. Steady repeat sales also help your listing hold its place.

Step 1: Audit your digital shelf

When Hill's Science Diet launched on Amazon.ca, it didn't take off. Instead of spending more on ads, we stopped and audited the shelf: how shoppers find you, what they see when they land, and whether they trust you enough to subscribe. That audit found the fixes that unlocked the growth.

Step 2: Buy the right traffic

Once the shelf converts, turn on search ads. Defend your brand name, go after need-based searches such as "sensitive stomach dog food", and test competitor terms with care. Judge the cost against what a repeat customer is worth, not just the first order.

Step 3: Reward subscribing, not one-off deals

Deep one-off discounts teach shoppers to wait for the next deal. Put your promo money behind subscribing instead, so the discount earns you a repeat customer.

Step 4: Track the right numbers

  • Share of sales from Subscribe & Save
  • Repeat rate and how long subscribers stay
  • Ad cost compared with repeat customer value
  • In-stock rate, rating and review count

The lesson

Fix the shelf before you buy traffic. Sending ads to a weak listing burns money. Read the full Hill's Science Diet case study.

Common questions

Does the same approach work on Chewy Autoship?

Yes. The order is the same: a strong product page, the right traffic, then offers that reward repeat orders.

How long before Subscribe & Save grows?

Shelf fixes can lift conversion within weeks. Subscription share builds over a few months as new customers reorder.
