Global Pet Expo 2027: how to turn the show into retail orders

Ahmed RehmanEx-Nestlé Purina, Hill's, Moose Toys · Updated November 25, 2026
Short answer

Global Pet Expo 2027 runs March 17 to 19, 2027, at the Orange County Convention Center in Orlando. The brands that get orders from it book buyer and distributor meetings weeks ahead, bring a one-page sell sheet with retail-ready pricing and proof, and follow up within two days with a clear next step. Treat the booth as the start of the sale, not the sale itself.

The basics

Global Pet Expo is the biggest pet industry trade show in North America. It's presented by the American Pet Products Association (APPA) and the Pet Industry Distributors Association (PIDA). The 2027 show runs March 17 to 19, 2027, at the Orange County Convention Center in Orlando, Florida, with The Pet Summit running alongside it from March 15. It is trade-only, so it is not open to the public.

Buyers from pet specialty chains, independent stores, online retailers, distributors and international markets all attend. That's why it's worth planning for, whether or not you're exhibiting.

Before the show: book the meetings

  • Pick your targets. List the 20 to 30 retailers and distributors that matter most for your next year, and why.
  • Reach out six to eight weeks ahead. Buyers' calendars fill fast. Ask for 15 minutes with a specific reason: a new item, a sales result, a gap in their set.
  • Know the distributors. Many independent pet stores buy through distributors, so a distributor meeting can open hundreds of doors at once.
  • Get your numbers ready. Wholesale and suggested retail prices, margins, case packs, minimums and lead times.

What to bring

  • A one-page sell sheet for each item: who it's for, why it wins, price, margin and proof.
  • Sales proof: velocity at current stores, Amazon or Chewy ranks, review scores and repeat rates.
  • A 90-day launch plan showing how you'll send shoppers to their stores.
  • Samples that are easy to carry, and a simple way to capture each conversation and next step.

At the show

Keep the pitch short. Lead with the shopper problem and your proof, not your origin story. Ask every buyer what they need to see before saying yes, and write it down. Walk the floor too: see what's launching in your category so you know what you're up against before the next line review. If you're entering the New Products Showcase, check the show's site for rules and deadlines.

After the show: where the orders come from

Most brands lose the value of the show in the two weeks after it. Follow up within 48 hours with a short note that repeats what the buyer asked for and the next step. Send the item setup paperwork quickly if they're interested, and put dates in the calendar for the next check-in. A trade show gets you the conversation. Your follow up, pricing and launch plan get you the order. For what buyers weigh when they decide, read how to get into PetSmart, Petco and Walmart.

Common questions

When and where is Global Pet Expo 2027?

March 17 to 19, 2027, at the Orange County Convention Center in Orlando, Florida. The Pet Summit runs alongside it from March 15.

Is Global Pet Expo open to the public?

No. It is a trade-only show for retailers, distributors, manufacturers and press.

Is it worth going to Global Pet Expo if I'm not exhibiting?

It can be, if you book meetings ahead and register as the right kind of attendee. Without booked meetings, it's mostly a market tour.
